# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- New look from the game itself: HUD pieces are ruled grey forms like the Tenement drawing (speckled paper, ink rules, spaced typewriter capitals), in the game's colours.
- Needs strip as a title block; health and mind in octagon badges; warnings as ruled tickets; wallet as a ledger; notifications, item cards and the menu status sheet as forms.
- Menus use the drawing-sheet grey with ruled edges instead of cardboard and manila; the open tab is the game's amber.
- Fixed: the HUD covered the building-mode furniture bar (L); it hides while the building menu is open.

## 1.0.6
- Fixed: the status clipboard in menus (and the watch and receipt) covered the game's skill level-up popup on the right. They step aside while it shows.

## 1.0.5
- Fixed: lowering Scale moved the HUD toward the middle of the screen. The needs strip, tags and slips now stay at the screen edges at any Scale, and the menu clipboard stays beside the inventory panel.
- The game's look-at text (item name, amount) is lifted above the needs strip instead of hiding behind it.
- Paper menus: vending machine rows are paper with readable names and prices; action buttons (Buy, Sell, Use, Equip...) keep the game's brown buttons with light text; the note title bar matches the Tasks title bars.

## 1.0.4
- Item panel buttons (Buy, Sell, Use, Eat...) keep the game's look with readable text in the paper menus, also in trade screens.
- Paper menu text gets its colour right away: no white flash when a menu, page or storage opens.
- The HUD hides while another mod's window is open (Scam Artist's ledger, Special Orders...).

## 1.0.3
- Paper menus: text colours follow panels the game switches between light and dark (Items page section headers, Tasks title bars), so they no longer fade.
- Paper menus: highlight rows on the Items page lie on paper instead of showing dark cardboard bands.
- The menu clipboard, watch and receipt step aside while an item tooltip would overlap them.

## 1.0.2
- Warning stamps (Police, Fungus, Rad...) are now stamped on paper, so they stay readable on dark scenes and at night.

## 1.0.1
- Fixed the label of item action buttons (Eat, Slaughter, Drink...) turning invisible with the paper menus: text on the game's own buttons keeps its colour.

## 1.0.0
- First release: needs on masking tape, health and mind dials, warning stamps, LCD wristwatch, receipt wallet with payment lines, typed notification slips, equipped-item luggage tags, status clipboard, folder tabs and storage box labels in menus.
- Paper menus: cardboard frames, manila rows and cards with ink text (Tasks, Notes, Character, inventory lists); colours only, fully restorable.
- Bank Inventory and Loot Map panels switch to the paper look when installed (each optional).
- Hidden on loading screens, the pause menu and during conversations; follows the game's own HUD visibility everywhere else.
- No false "+ received" lines when a save loads.
