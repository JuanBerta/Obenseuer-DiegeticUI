=== FULL DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ===
[size=5][b]Diegetic UI[/b][/size]
[i]The HUD becomes the things you carry.[/i]

Obenseuer is a gritty, grounded game, so this HUD is too: instead of floating icons, everything you need to know is written, printed or stamped on things your character has on them.

[size=4][b]On screen[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Needs on masking tape[/b]: Food, Water, Rest, Toilet and Clean as ink gauges (higher is better). They go from green through olive and mustard to rust and red, and pulse when nearly empty.
[*][b]Health and Mind[/b] on needle dials printed on paper cards.
[*][b]Warnings as rubber stamps[/b]: Bleeding, Police (the real crime value), Arrest, Radiation, Fungus, the cravings and the duck. Severe ones throb.
[*][b]A cheap LCD wristwatch[/b]: the time only shows with a watch equipped, the weekday only with a calendar watch - the game's own rule.
[*][b]A receipt wallet[/b]: OC and RM, and every payment prints a green or red line that fades away.
[*][b]Typed paper slips[/b] for notifications, coloured by category. The game's own queue, sounds and timing are kept.
[*][b]Luggage tags[/b] for your equipped items, with the real item icon, battery segments for lights and a wear bar for tools.
[/list]

[size=4][b]In menus[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Paper menus[/b]: dark frames become cardboard, rows and cards become manila paper with ink text - Tasks, Notes, the Character skill cards, the inventory lists. Only colours change, so everything works as before; item slots, crafting panels and the Tenement blueprint keep the game's look.
[*]A [b]status clipboard[/b] in the free space beside the inventory, live while you eat or drink.
[*]A compact watch and receipt.
[*][b]Manila folder tabs[/b] and a paper label on storage headers ("BANK · box 2 of 4" with Bank Inventory).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Display only: it reads the game's values and hides the game's HUD pieces it replaces. Needs, money and saves are untouched.
[*]Hidden on loading screens, the pause menu, conversations, building mode and whenever the game hides its own HUD.
[*]Everything can be turned on or off, scaled and faded in the config: [code]BepInEx\config\com.juanberta.obenseuer.diegeticui.cfg[/code]
[*]Use only one HUD replacement (it warns you if UI Overhaul is installed too).
[*][b]Bank Inventory[/b] and [b]Loot Map[/b] switch their own panels to the paper look when this mod is installed (each can be turned off).
[*]Works with Recipe Marker, Special Orders and the skill mods.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]BepInEx 6[/b] (x64).
[*]Extract the archive into your Obenseuer folder (where Obenseuer.exe is).
[*]Remove any other HUD replacement mod.
[/list]
