=== NAME ===
Diegetic UI

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (summary) ===
A HUD and menus drawn like the game's own Tenement blueprint: ruled grey forms, typewriter labels, octagon dials for health and mind, a cheap LCD watch, a wallet ledger, form notifications and item cards.

=== FILE DESCRIPTION ===
Obenseuer-DiegeticUI-v1.1.0.rar : Diegetic UI 1.1.0 for BepInEx 6. Extract into the Obenseuer folder.

=== CHANGELOG (Nexus "Changelogs" tab) ===
1.1.0
- New look, taken from the game itself: every HUD piece is a ruled grey form like the Tenement page's drawing (speckled paper, 2px ink rules, spaced typewriter capitals), in the game's own colours.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ===
[size=5][b]Diegetic UI[/b][/size]
[i]A HUD that looks like it belongs in Obenseuer.[/i]

Everything on screen is drawn like the game's own Tenement blueprint: speckled grey drawing paper, ruled ink cells, spaced typewriter capitals and the game's own colours (olive, amber and blood red for good, low and critical). No floating icons.

[size=4][b]On screen[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Needs strip[/b]: a title block along the bottom with a cell for Food, Water, Rest, Toilet and Clean (name, value and a ruled bar), and Health and Mind in octagon badges with a fill ring. Critical needs pulse.
[*][b]Warning tickets[/b]: Bleeding, Police (the real crime value), Arrest, Radiation, Fungus, the cravings and the duck, each with its colour; severe ones throb.
[*][b]A cheap LCD wristwatch[/b]: the time only shows with a watch equipped, the weekday only with a calendar watch - the game's own rule.
[*][b]A wallet ledger[/b]: OC and RM, and every payment adds a green or red line that fades away.
[*][b]Notification forms[/b], coloured by category. The game's own queue, sounds and timing are kept.
[*][b]Item cards[/b] for what you hold, with the real item icon, its amount and a charge or wear bar.
[/list]

[size=4][b]In menus[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Drawing-sheet menus[/b]: frames, rows and cards take the grey paper with ruled edges and ink text - Tasks, Notes, the Character skill cards, the inventory lists, shops. Only colours change, so everything works as before; item slots, crafting panels and the Tenement page keep the game's look.
[*]A [b]status sheet[/b] beside the inventory (Condition and Notes tables), live while you eat or drink.
[*]A compact watch and wallet, paper tabs (the open one in the game's amber) and a ruled label on storage headers ("BANK · box 2 of 4" with Bank Inventory).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Display only: it reads the game's values and hides the game's HUD pieces it replaces. Needs, money and saves are untouched.
[*]Hidden on loading screens, the pause menu, conversations, building mode and whenever the game hides its own HUD.
[*]Everything can be turned on or off, scaled and faded in the config: [code]BepInEx\config\com.juanberta.obenseuer.diegeticui.cfg[/code]
[*]Use only one HUD replacement (it warns you if UI Overhaul is installed too).
[*][b]Bank Inventory[/b] and [b]Loot Map[/b] switch their own panels to the paper look when this mod is installed (each can be turned off).
[*]Works with Recipe Marker, Special Orders and the Skills Pack.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]BepInEx 6[/b] (x64).
[*]Extract the archive into your Obenseuer folder (where Obenseuer.exe is).
[*]Remove any other HUD replacement mod.
[/list]
